Meet the Author

Hello, I'm Christina!

I'm so Glad that you decided to check me out. Well Im Christina my friends call me chriss, Christy and so on. I am a teacher. I play guitar and can write songs. I am a big time music person and I want the world to know God as He truely is not just through what people say about him. Im perfectly imperfect according to worldly standards but I know just what I am in His eyes.

My Journey

What inspired me to start this. Its actually a funny story. I am a computer science student. So Iam something of a CS Engineer myself. With all the knowledge I have about websites it would be a waste of my wisdom if I didnt had a website for myself to host. All my life I had the episodes of falling down and getting up, looking for a place of comfort attention, and care and had my share of identity crisis moments. Well some of those genz kids out there thinking about lgbtq as soon as you read "Identity crisis" its not whatever you are thinking. When I say Identity crisis its struggle of acceptance between your identity as "SON" OR "ORPHAN" OR "SLAVE". Clearly we have recieved the spirit of adoption for that reason we call God "ABBA, Father". Till I realised there isnt a place of that sort, and I can find it only in one man and that is God. When I say God I ment Jesus alone. As God started changing my mindset through people in my life like teachers, friends etc etc, I realised putting it in internet as written form would help others struggling with same things and get empowerred with Grace.

Why THE NARROW GATE?

Most young people like me struggle with identity, purpose and understanding God. Since the foundation of knowledge about these things is mere we youngsters tend to see situations as a personal attack or as an unwinnable battle. Im here to change the reader's perspective, just like how God changed mine. I am blessed to have a wonderful teacher in my life—my pastor and mentor, through whose teachings I have come to understand God's true nature. Many of the thoughts shared on this blog are inspired by the powerful sermons I have had the privilege of listening to.

A Note to My Readers

When you read my writings, I hope you find answers to the questions you've been carrying. My prayer is that nothing I share leaves you feeling more confused or lost than before. Above all, I pray that you come to know the truth, for it is the truth that will set you free. Feel free to leave a comment and share your thoughts, questions, and personal experiences. I'd love to hear from you!
